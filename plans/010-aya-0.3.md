# AYA 0.3 — Continued Existence Register

Status: IMPLEMENTATION / EXTERNAL ACTIVATION GATED  
Branch: `feat/aya-0.3`  
Archive: `FLD-AYA-01`  
Institution: Federal Cartographic & Cultural Documentation Bureau (F.C.C.D.B.)

## Product thesis

Are You Alive? remains a single-purpose 39-hour check-in instrument. Version 0.3 folds
it into the ESTD. INDICA / F.C.C.D.B. system without turning it into a branded
companion app or a generic safety dashboard.

The app is the Bureau's proof-of-continued-existence filing instrument.

The connection to ESTD. INDICA should be discovered through shared institutional
grammar — filing blocks, archive codes, typography, status language, record extracts —
not announced through cross-promotion.

## Guardrails

1. Keep the public product name **Are You Alive?**
2. Keep the canonical interval at **39 hours**.
3. Keep the core action plain-language: **I'M ALIVE**.
4. Do not imply emergency-service monitoring.
5. Do not claim a paired contact will be alerted until a server-side watchdog actually
   exists and has been tested end-to-end.
6. Local-first remains the default. Network state exists only where needed for pairing
   and watchdog reliability.
7. ESTD. INDICA aesthetics must change the information architecture, not merely recolor
   the current UI.
8. The app and the website share structural logic, not identical layouts.

## Research basis

The 0.3 direction follows the September 2026 audit:
- modern dead-man-switch implementations treat server-side expiry as necessary for
  contact escalation when the subject phone is unavailable;
- archival digital services increasingly use progressive disclosure and plain actions
  while retaining institutional metadata and hierarchy;
- government design-system case studies favor shared structural/component logic over
  decorative mimicry;
- current mobile notification guidance requires graceful handling of platform alarm
  restrictions rather than assuming scheduled delivery is guaranteed.

Relevant references:
- The National Archives, "How the design of our new online catalogue puts users first"
  (2026-04-28)
- Beacon Safe, "Dead Man's Switch Apps Explained" (2026-04-24)
